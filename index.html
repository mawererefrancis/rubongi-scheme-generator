About this System

• Static & GitHub Pages ready — no server, no database, no PHP.
• All data is saved in your browser's localStorage.
• Scheme of work prints in A4 landscape; lesson plans and records print in A4 portrait.
• Word downloads carry the correct orientation via MS Word page settings.
• Lesson plans are per 2-period lesson (80 min each). 4 periods/week = 2 lesson plans/week.
• Custom topic selection (optional) lets you pick topics across any term to teach ahead of the syllabus.
• Curriculum data is embedded from official NCDC syllabi.
